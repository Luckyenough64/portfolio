I am a Lead Data Analyst at Ozon, one of the largest e-commerce platforms in the CIS.

I started in web analytics and marketing, working with product and behavioral data. Later, I moved toward data infrastructure, distributed systems, and analytics engineering.

Today my work sits between product analytics, experimentation, data engineering, AI tools, and systems research.

Metrics describe a change. The next step is to test which causes are consistent with it.
  • how systems behave as load grows
  • which costs are hidden by analytical abstractions
  • how to define evaluation criteria for conversational AI
  • how to interpret A/B test results
  • how to design reproducible pipelines
